Legionella Control and Prevention Protocol 2024

Dr. Sarah Chen

Lead Bio-Safety Compliance Officer • Updated 2 days ago

Falkon Care follows strict HSG274 guidelines to ensure all water systems remain free of Legionella bacteria. This protocol outlines the specific steps our technicians take during every site visit.

1. Temperature Monitoring

Bacteria thrive in temperatures between 20°C and 45°C. We maintain hot water at a minimum of 60°C and cold water below 20°C to prevent colonization.

Critical Insight:

Regular "flushing" of little-used outlets is the single most effective way to prevent biofilm buildup in commercial plumbing systems.

2. Chemical Disinfection

Our teams use high-grade Chlorine Dioxide (ClO2) which is 10x more effective than traditional chlorine against biofilms. Our dosage levels are precisely calculated based on tank volume.

3. Reporting & Compliance

Every test result is logged in real-time to your Falkon Care Dashboard. Digital signatures from our lead technicians ensure a legally compliant audit trail for your health and safety records.
