# Falkon Care - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview
**Product Name:** Falkon Care  
**Tagline:** Pure Water. Pure Professionalism.  
**Vision:** To become the #1 professional water hygiene and tank cleaning service platform in the Delhi NCR region, bridging the gap between utility services and high-end digital SaaS experiences.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
*   **Hygiene Risks:** Unprofessional cleaning leads to waterborne diseases and contamination.
*   **Trust Deficit:** Lack of verified technicians and transparent pricing in the local market.
*   **Operational Friction:** Traditional booking methods are slow, manual, and offer no real-time tracking.
*   **Compliance Gaps:** Commercial clients lack centralized logs for hygiene certification and safety standards.

---

## 3. Goals & Objectives
*   **Seamless Booking:** Enable residential and commercial users to book certified cleaning in < 2 minutes.
*   **Operational Transparency:** Provide real-time tracking of technicians and digital "Certificates of Sterility."
*   **Scalability:** Build a multi-tenant architecture supporting individual homes, RWA societies, and corporate clients.
*   **Brand Authority:** Establish a "Hydro-SaaS" aesthetic that communicates precision and safety.

---

## 4. Target Audience
1.  **Residential Homeowners:** Health-conscious families seeking reliable home maintenance.
2.  **RWA & Society Managers:** Decision-makers overseeing large-scale community water systems.
3.  **Commercial/Corporate Clients:** Hotels, hospitals, and offices requiring strict compliance and recurring maintenance (AMC).
4.  **Admins/Operators:** Internal team managing dispatch, staff availability, and revenue growth.

---

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 User Features (Customer Portal)
*   **Service Catalog:** Browse specialized services (Tank Sterilization, Pipe Flushing, Lab Testing).
*   **Smart Booking Form:** Multi-step intake capturing Aadhaar, DOB (auto-calc age), and precise tank specifications.
*   **Live Tracker:** GPS-enabled technician tracking with estimated time of arrival (ETA).
*   **Wallet System:** Pre-paid credit management for quick one-tap bookings.
*   **Activity Log:** Historical record of cleanings with downloadable PDF hygiene reports.

### 5.2 Admin Features (Operational Panel)
*   **Real-time Dashboard:** Monitoring active appointments, fleet efficiency, and response times.
*   **Revenue Analytics:** Weekly/monthly growth tracking with data visualization.
*   **Staff Management:** Real-time availability toggles and technician performance ratings.
*   **Global Booking Management:** Centralized dispatch and assignment interface.

---

## 6. Design System: "Aquatic Precision"
*   **Core Aesthetic:** Minimalist, clinical, and professional.
*   **Color Palette:** Trust Blue (#0284C7), Sterile Teal, and soft slate grays.
*   **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans for modern clarity.
*   **UI Patterns:** Soft-shadowed cards, rounded corners (8px), and high-contrast status badges (Completed, In Progress, Pending).

---

## 7. Technology Stack
*   **Frontend:** Next.js 16 (App Router) for high-performance SSR and SEO.
*   **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + Radix UI for accessible, responsive components.
*   **Backend/Database:** Convex for real-time data sync and reactive queries.
*   **Authentication:** Clerk for secure, multi-role (User/Admin) management.
*   **Analytics:** Integrated performance and revenue tracking.

---

## 8. Roadmap & Future Scope
*   **Phase 1 (Current):** Core booking engine, Admin/Customer dashboards, and Landing Page.
*   **Phase 2:** Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and automated subscription billing.
*   **Phase 3:** Mobile app launch (iOS/Android) and IoT integration for real-time water quality monitoring.
*   **Phase 4:** Regional expansion beyond Delhi NCR to other Indian metros.
